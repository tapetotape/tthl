# Coming Soon

The **Tape to Tape Hockey League (TTHL)** is a competitive **6v6 EASHL league for NHL 27**, featuring cross-platform competition between **Xbox Series X|S and PlayStation 5**. 
<br>
Our league is built around **competitive hockey, integrity, sportsmanship, fair play, and giving our players and owners a voice in the direction of the league.**

<br>
